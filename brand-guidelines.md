# **Fe-Dev — Brand Guidelines**

---

## **1. Brand Overview**

**Brand Name:** Fe-Dev  
**Founder:** Federico Lardieri  
**Brand Type:** AI & Automation Studio  

Fe-Dev is a **modern, premium, technology-driven brand** focused on helping businesses **scale through AI, automation and custom software**.

The brand sits at the intersection of:
- Artificial Intelligence
- Business Automation
- Web Development
- Internal Tools
- SaaS Products

Fe-Dev is **not** a generic freelancer brand  
Fe-Dev is **not** a classic digital agency  

Fe-Dev is a **technical partner** that builds systems.

---

## **2. Brand Mission**

Help companies:

- Reduce manual work
- Increase operational efficiency
- Scale processes and revenue
- Make better decisions through data and automation

By building:
- AI-powered automations
- Custom web applications
- Internal tools
- Scalable SaaS solutions

Fe-Dev focuses on **leverage**, not hours.

---

## **3. Brand Positioning**

Fe-Dev positions itself as:

> **An AI-driven engineer who builds systems that work even when the business owner is not working.**

Key concepts:
- Scalability
- Engineering mindset
- Business understanding
- Long-term solutions

---

## **4. Brand Personality**

The brand must feel:

- **Professional**, never corporate
- **Confident**, never arrogant
- **Highly technical**, but understandable
- **Modern**, clean and future-oriented

Tone of voice:
- Clear
- Direct
- Solution-oriented
- No hype
- No buzzwords without substance

---

## **5. Target Audience**

### **Primary**
- Small & medium businesses
- Entrepreneurs
- Agencies
- Local companies that want to scale

### **Secondary**
- Startups
- Founders
- Professionals looking for automation

Audience mindset:
- They want results
- They value speed
- They want clarity
- They want a technical partner, not a salesman

---

## **6. Visual Identity**

### **Color Palette**

**Primary Brand Color**
- Deep Violet / Purple  
- Example HEX: `#7A3EF0`

**Background**
- Dark / near-black  
- Example HEX: `#0B0B0F` or `#111118`

**Text Colors**
- Primary text: White / Light gray  
- Secondary text: Muted gray

**Accent**
- Violet glow
- Subtle gradients
- Never bright or playful colors

**Usage Rules**
- Dark UI only
- High contrast
- Glow used sparingly and intentionally

---

### **Typography**

**Primary Font Style**
- Modern, geometric sans-serif

Suggested fonts:
- Inter
- Poppins
- SF Pro style fonts

Rules:
- Clear hierarchy
- Large headings
- Generous spacing
- Readability over style

---

## **7. Design Style**

Overall design must be:

- Dark themed
- Minimal
- Premium
- Tech-oriented

Key elements:
- Cards with soft shadows
- Slightly rounded corners
- Subtle glow accents
- Smooth transitions

Avoid:
- Visual clutter
- Long paragraphs
- Stock images
- Over-designed layouts

---

## **8. Website Goals**

The website must:

1. Position Fe-Dev as a **serious AI & automation partner**
2. Explain clearly **what problems are solved**
3. Showcase **capabilities**, not generic services
4. Drive the user to **contact or book a call**

---

## **9. Core Website Structure**

### **Hero Section**

The hero must communicate in under 5 seconds:

- Who I am
- What I do
- Why it matters

Suggested concept:
> *“I build AI-powered systems that help businesses scale.”*

Primary CTA:
- Book a call
- Get in touch

---

## **10. Interactive Landing Showcase (Hero Feature)**

### **Purpose**

The landing page **MUST** include a **dynamic, interactive showcase**.

Its purpose is to:
- **Instantly communicate technical competence**
- **Convey modern engineering and premium quality**
- **Make the website feel alive and intelligent**

This element is **NOT decorative**.  
It is a **core identity feature** of the Fe-Dev brand.

---

### **Interaction & Motion Guidelines**

The showcase must react to **cursor movement**.

Allowed interactions:
- *Light 3D tilt* following mouse position
- *Parallax depth* between elements
- *Soft violet glow* reacting to hover
- *Smooth easing* and controlled motion

Motion must feel:
- Intentional
- Elegant
- Technical
- Never playful or exaggerated

---

### **Visual Concepts (Choose One)**

**Option 1 — 3D Tech Dashboard (Recommended)**
- Central dashboard UI mockup
- Represents:
  - AI systems
  - Automations
  - Data & analytics
- Perspective shifts with cursor
- Glow accents on key blocks

**Option 2 — Modular System Cards**
- 3–4 layered cards
- Each card represents:
  - AI Automation
  - Web Apps
  - SaaS
  - Integrations
- Independent depth & tilt

**Option 3 — Data Flow Architecture**
- Nodes and connections
- Lines illuminate on hover
- Multiple depth layers
- Visual metaphor for scalable systems

---

### **Technical Constraints**

- Performance-first
- No lag
- No continuous loops
- Cursor-based interaction only

**Mobile behavior**
- Disable heavy interaction
- Use static or light animation
- Preserve premium feel

---

### **What to Avoid**

- ❌ Flashy or gaming effects  
- ❌ Distracting animations  
- ❌ Overuse of motion  
- ❌ Decorative visuals without meaning  

---

### **Design Intent**

The user should think:

> *“This person builds real systems, not just websites.”*

---

## **11. Services / Capabilities Section**

Present services as **solutions**, not tasks.

Examples:
- AI Automations
- Business Process Automation
- Custom Web Applications
- Internal Tools
- SaaS Development
- API Integrations

Focus on outcomes, not features.

---

## **12. How Fe-Dev Helps Businesses Scale**

Explain clearly:
- Reduction of manual work
- Faster operations
- Better decision-making
- Increased margins

Use concrete examples, not abstract promises.

---

## **13. Projects / Use Cases**

Show:
- Systems
- Tools
- Automations

Even conceptual examples are acceptable if presented as **use cases**.

---

## **14. Contact & Call to Action**

- Clear CTA
- Minimal form
- No friction

Goal:
- Book a call
- Start a conversation

---

## **15. Technical Preferences**

- Modern frontend stack
- Component-based structure
- Mobile-first
- SEO-friendly
- Fast loading

Prefer:
- Minimal dependencies
- Clean code
- Maintainability

---

## **16. What to Avoid (Global Rules)**

- Overpromising results
- Marketing hype
- Emojis
- Buzzwords without explanation
- Cheap or playful visuals

---

## **17. Final Instruction**

These guidelines are **HARD CONSTRAINTS**.

The website must feel like:
- A premium AI & automation studio
- A technical partner
- A long-term collaborator

**Not**
- A freelancer portfolio
- A generic agency website

---

## **Brand Essence**

> **Clean. Dark. Intelligent. Scalable.**
